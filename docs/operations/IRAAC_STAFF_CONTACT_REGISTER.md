## Approved operational uses

1. Notify every member whenever a new or revised community, government or
   IRAAC/NGO/partner staff report and proposed email is ready for review.
2. Send every active member the final approved version of all three report
   families unless an applicable unsubscribe, suppression, removal or legal
   hold blocks delivery.
3. Seed these contacts into Path 2 with provenance
   `staff_affiliate_seed_2026_07_31`.
4. Send one deduplicated monthly consultation asking what IRAAC should add,
   improve or investigate across the website, survey, reports and operations.

## Governance rules

- Membership does not create SMS, human-call or AI-call consent.
- Deliver review and report messages individually or through a managed group;
  do not expose the full address list in visible recipient headers.
- Email replies are comments or suggestions only. Formal report approval occurs
  in the protected, named-account, MFA-backed Approvals dashboard.
- Every material report or email change creates a successor version,
  invalidates affected approvals and triggers a fresh review notification.
- Membership additions, removals and corrections must record the named
  administrator, reason, effective date and previous/new register version.
- Change requests route through info@iraac-aco.com.
